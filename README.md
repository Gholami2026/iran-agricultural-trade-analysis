# Agricultural Trade and Export Diversification in Iran

## Project Overview

This research project examines the structure, performance, and evolution of Iran's agricultural exports using international trade data and quantitative analytical methods. It focuses on export diversification, revealed comparative advantage, export stability, competitiveness, and changes in export performance across products and destination markets.

The project aims to develop an empirical understanding of Iran's agricultural export patterns and identify how the composition and performance of agricultural exports have changed over time.

## Research Objectives

The main objectives are to:

- Examine the structure and evolution of Iran's agricultural exports.
- Analyze export diversification across agricultural products and destination markets.
- Evaluate revealed comparative advantage and its persistence over time.
- Investigate export stability and resilience across products and markets.
- Assess export competitiveness and changes in export unit values.
- Explore the relationship between export patterns and broader economic and environmental challenges.

## Data and Scope

The project uses international bilateral trade data from the BACI international trade database, developed by CEPII.

The research framework considers Iran's agricultural exports across products, destination markets, and years. The planned analytical period covers 1995–2024, subject to data availability and the inclusion criteria applied in each analysis.

The product universe is defined through a documented selection and screening process to support consistency and transparency in the empirical analysis.

## Research Themes

### 1. Agricultural Export Structure

Descriptive analysis of Iran's agricultural exports across products, destination markets, and years.

### 2. Export Diversification

Examination of the composition of agricultural exports and the distribution of exports across products and destination markets.

### 3. Revealed Comparative Advantage

Assessment of comparative advantage using trade-based indicators, including the Revealed Comparative Advantage (RCA) index and related robustness measures.

### 4. Export Stability and Resilience

Analysis of changes in export performance over time, including the stability of export values, quantities, and unit values.

### 5. Export Competitiveness

Investigation of export performance and unit-value dynamics across agricultural products and international markets.

### 6. Economic and Environmental Risk

Development of a broader research perspective on the potential relationship between export performance, external shocks, and climate-related risks.

## Data and Methods

The project combines international trade data processing with quantitative analysis.

The methodological framework may include:

- Data cleaning and consistency checks.
- Product-level and destination-level export analysis.
- Descriptive statistics and trend analysis.
- Revealed Comparative Advantage (RCA).
- Symmetric Revealed Comparative Advantage (RSCA) and Normalized Revealed Comparative Advantage (NRCA) as complementary indicators.
- Export growth and stability measures.
- Unit-value analysis based on export values and quantities.
- Robustness checks and sensitivity analysis where appropriate.

The specific methods and indicators are selected according to the research question and the characteristics of the data used in each component.
